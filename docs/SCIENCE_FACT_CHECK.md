# Scientific Source Ledger & Fact-Check — RoverQuest: Beyond the Silent Machines

**Reviewed:** November 13, 2026  
**Scope:** Claim-level scientific audit across the 9 Mars places (Sojourner, Spirit & Opportunity, Viking 1 & 2, Phoenix & InSight, Ingenuity, and the Dawn, NEAR Shoemaker, Pioneer 10 & 11 and Kepler holo-beacons; any 5 required) and the 4 Moon sites (any 3 required), 32 locally bundled NASA hardware/evidence photographs, and NSSDCA Master Catalog records.

> **Epistemological Rule Enforced Across the App:** Every investigation separates **(1) Documented Mission Observations** (e.g., NSSDCA records, surface photographs, measured mineral signatures) from **(2) Scientific Interpretations** (e.g., ancient acidic groundwater, hydrothermal alteration, liquid core) and **(3) Explicit Teaching Simplifications** (`🧪 SYNTHETIC TEACHING DATA — NOT RAW NASA TELEMETRY`). Fictional archive voices are labelled as educational storytelling reconstructions, and 3D meshes are labelled as `Procedural Educational Approximations (Not Engineering CAD)`.
